Colorado Homeschool Law

Know Your Rights

Compulsory Age

Age 6 by Aug 1

Annual Requirement

172 days / 688 hrs

Colorado homeschooling families must fulfill the state's compulsory attendance requirements by reporting either to a public school district or to a private independent school like CHESS.

Homeschool Curriculum

Curriculum Resources

A note from Colorado Heritage: The curriculum publishers and resources listed below are included as a starting point for families. Their inclusion on this page does not imply that Colorado Heritage has personally reviewed or endorsed every product or resource they offer. We encourage parents to prayerfully evaluate all curriculum using their own best judgment and the needs of their child.

Math-U-See

mathusee.com

A mastery-based, manipulative-driven math curriculum designed for homeschoolers. Covers pre-K through calculus with a step-by-step approach that helps students truly understand mathematical concepts.

Visit Site

BJU Press Homeschool

bjupresshomeschool.com

A complete K–12 Christian curriculum from Bob Jones University, covering all core subjects with a biblical worldview woven throughout. Known for rigorous academics and teacher-friendly materials.

Visit Site

Abeka

abeka.com

A traditional, phonics-based, Christian curriculum originally developed by Pensacola Christian College. Offers K–12 materials in all subjects, available in print or video format, with a structured and rigorous approach.

Visit Site

Apologia

apologia.com

Award-winning Christian science and worldview curriculum. Their science courses are widely used in homeschool co-ops and are written from a young-earth creationist perspective. Also offers Bible, history, and language arts.

Visit Site

Classical Conversations

classicalconversations.com

A classical, Christian homeschool community and curriculum model based on the Trivium (Grammar, Dialectic, Rhetoric). Emphasizes memory, critical thinking, and a Christian worldview through community and conversation.

Visit Site

Sonlight

sonlight.com

A literature-rich, Christian homeschool curriculum built around great books, world history, and a biblical framework. Known for its read-aloud approach and internationally-minded perspective on history and culture.

Visit Site
