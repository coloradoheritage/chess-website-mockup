Why Give

Making a Difference Together

Colorado Heritage Education School System is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Your donations are tax-deductible and directly support our mission to serve homeschooling families with excellence, integrity, and biblical conviction.

Tax-Deductible Contributions

As a 501(c)(3) organization, your gifts to Colorado Heritage Education School System are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. A receipt will be provided for all donations.

General Fund

Supports the day-to-day operations of Colorado Heritage — office staff, administrative costs, communications, and the infrastructure that keeps our programs running.

How to Give

Contact our office to donate to the General Fund.

Scholarship Fund

Helps make Academy classes and programs accessible to families who need financial assistance. Every gift to this fund directly reduces barriers to Christian education.

How to Give

Contact our office to donate to the Scholarship Fund.

Capital & Facilities

Supports facility improvements, equipment, and long-term investments in the physical resources our Academy families depend on.

How to Give

Contact our office to donate to the Capital Fund.
