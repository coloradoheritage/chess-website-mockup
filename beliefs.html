Our Foundation

Why We Exist

"The Bible itself, as the inspired and infallible Word of God that speaks with final authority concerning truth, morality, and the proper conduct of mankind, is the sole and final source of all that we believe."

Colorado Heritage Independent School & Academy exists because we believe that all truth is God's truth and that education must be grounded in the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

We affirm the historic Christian faith as confessed by the church throughout the centuries in the Apostles' Creed, the Nicene Creed, the Athanasian Creed, the Definition of Chalcedon, the Protestant Reformation confessions, and the faithful evangelical tradition.

We believe Christian education is not religious instruction added onto a secular foundation, but the joyful pursuit of all knowledge under the authority of God's Word.

Because of this, we seek to partner with families who desire to raise their children in the nurture and admonition of the Lord, forming students intellectually, morally, spiritually, and culturally according to a distinctly Christian worldview.

What We Believe

Article I

Concerning God

We believe there is one true and living God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — equal in power and glory, of one essence, eternal, uncreated, holy, sovereign, and worthy of all worship.

God is the Creator and Sustainer of all things. He governs all creation according to His wisdom and providence and works all things according to the counsel of His will.

We believe God is perfectly holy, perfectly just, perfectly loving, and perfectly good.

Article II

Concerning Holy Scripture

We believe the Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God, breathed out by God Himself through human authors.

The Bible is true, trustworthy, without error in all that it affirms, and the supreme authority for faith, doctrine, morality, and life.

We believe Scripture is sufficient for knowing God, understanding salvation, and equipping believers for faithful living.

Because God has spoken, truth is objective, knowable, and binding upon all people.

Article III

Concerning Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man.

We believe:

  • He was conceived by the Holy Spirit,
  • Born of the virgin Mary,
  • Lived a sinless life,
  • Fulfilled the Law of God perfectly,
  • Performed mighty works,
  • Was crucified under Pontius Pilate,
  • Died as a substitutionary sacrifice for sinners,
  • Was buried,
  • Rose bodily from the dead on the third day,
  • Ascended into heaven,
  • And now reigns at the right hand of the Father as Lord and King.

We believe Jesus Christ will personally and visibly return to judge the living and the dead and to consummate His everlasting kingdom.

We affirm that salvation is found in Christ alone and that there is no other mediator between God and man.

Article IV

Concerning the Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is fully God and actively works in creation, revelation, salvation, sanctification, and the life of the church.

The Holy Spirit convicts the world of sin, regenerates sinners, indwells believers, grants spiritual gifts, illuminates Scripture, and empowers Christians for holy living and faithful witness.

Article V

Concerning Humanity

We believe all human beings are created in the image of God and therefore possess inherent dignity and value from conception to natural death.

God created mankind male and female. We believe these distinctions are good, purposeful, and established by God.

Because of Adam's fall into sin, all people are sinners by nature and by choice and are separated from God apart from His saving grace.

Humanity's greatest need is reconciliation to God through Jesus Christ.

Article VI

Concerning Salvation

We believe salvation is entirely by the grace of God through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

No person can earn salvation through moral effort, religious works, or personal righteousness.

We believe Christ's atoning death fully satisfied the justice of God for all who trust in Him.

Those who repent of their sins and place their faith in Christ are forgiven, declared righteous before God, adopted into His family, and granted eternal life.

We believe true faith results in a transformed life marked by repentance, obedience, love for God, and perseverance in the faith.

Article VII

Concerning the Church

We believe the universal church consists of all true believers united to Christ by faith.

Christ has ordained the local church for worship, discipleship, fellowship, the preaching of the Word, the administration of baptism and the Lord's Supper, prayer, and gospel witness.

We affirm the importance of faithful church membership, Christian fellowship, and submission to biblical authority within the church.

Article VIII

Concerning Marriage, Family, and Human Sexuality

We believe God established marriage as a covenant union exclusively between one biological man and one biological woman, for life. Marriage is not a human institution subject to redefinition, but a divine ordinance reflecting God's created order and His covenant relationship with His people.

We hold that the marriage covenant is binding for life (Romans 7:2–3), and that divorce is a departure from God's original design. We recognize, however, the exception our Lord Jesus Christ Himself provided — that divorce is permitted in the case of sexual immorality (Matthew 19:9) — and we extend grace and compassion to those who have experienced the pain of broken marriages, while upholding the permanence and sanctity of the marriage covenant as Scripture teaches.

The family is ordained by God as the foundational institution of human society.

Parents bear the primary responsibility before God for the nurture, education, discipline, and discipleship of their children.

We believe sexual intimacy is reserved by God for marriage alone and that God calls all people to purity, holiness, self-control, and repentance from sexual sin.

We affirm the dignity and worth of every human being while holding firmly to biblical truth regarding human sexuality, marriage, and morality.

Article IX

Concerning Education

We believe education is fundamentally the formation of the whole person under the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

Because God is the Creator of all things, all truth finds its source, meaning, and coherence in Him. Therefore, education can never be religiously neutral. Every subject — including mathematics, science, literature, history, government, economics, agriculture, music, and the arts — must ultimately be understood in relation to God and His created order.

We believe "the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom" (Prov. 9:10 ESV), and that true education must seek not merely the accumulation of information, but the cultivation of wisdom, virtue, discernment, diligence, and Christian character.

The purpose of Christian education is not merely academic achievement or vocational success, though both are important. Rather, Christian education seeks to train students to glorify God and faithfully fulfill their callings in every sphere of life.

We believe parents bear the primary responsibility before God for the nurture, discipline, and education of their children (Deut. 6:6–7; Eph. 6:4). Therefore, the role of the school is not to replace the family, but to come alongside parents as a supportive partner in the education and discipleship of their children.

We believe students should be trained:

  • To think biblically and carefully,
  • To love truth and reject falsehood,
  • To work diligently and joyfully,
  • To pursue excellence for the glory of God,
  • To serve faithfully in church, family, vocation, and society,
  • And to understand that all lawful work may be done unto the Lord.

We affirm that education includes both academic and spiritual formation. Students should be challenged intellectually while also being encouraged toward holiness, integrity, humility, responsibility, and love for God and neighbor.

We believe work itself is a gift from God and part of mankind's created purpose. Therefore, education should help students discover and develop their God-given gifts, abilities, and vocations so they may faithfully serve Christ in whatever calling He places before them.

We believe Christian education should cultivate:

  • Academic excellence,
  • Strong moral character,
  • Biblical wisdom,
  • Love for the local church,
  • Respect for lawful authority,
  • Meaningful vocational preparation,
  • And a lifelong commitment to learning under God.

Because all people are made in the image of God yet affected by sin, we recognize that students require both instruction and shepherding. Discipline, accountability, counseling, encouragement, and mentorship are therefore important aspects of Christian education when exercised with wisdom, grace, and truth.

We believe the ultimate goal of education is not self-fulfillment, worldly success, or social prestige, but faithfulness to God.

Accordingly, we seek to educate students who:

  • Love the Lord Jesus Christ,
  • Honor their parents,
  • Think with wisdom and discernment,
  • Work with diligence and integrity,
  • Value truth, goodness, and beauty,
  • And live as faithful Christians in every area of life.

"For from Him and through Him and to Him are all things. To Him be glory forever. Amen."

— Romans 11:36 (ESV)

Article X

Concerning Christian Living

We believe Christians are called to live in holiness, humility, love, integrity, diligence, courage, and truth.

Believers should seek to glorify God in every area of life — including family life, work, education, citizenship, worship, and culture.

We believe the Christian life is marked by ongoing repentance, growth in grace, obedience to Scripture, and dependence upon the Holy Spirit.

Article XI

Concerning the Last Things

We believe Jesus Christ will return bodily and visibly in glory.

There will be a final resurrection, final judgment, everlasting blessedness for the redeemed, and everlasting separation from God for the unrepentant.

We believe the kingdoms of this world ultimately belong to our Lord and to His Christ, and that Christ shall reign forever.

Our Conviction

We Joyfully Confess

We do not claim perfection, but we joyfully confess the historic Christian faith once delivered to the saints.

Our desire is to educate students who:

  • Love God and His truth,
  • Think carefully and biblically,
  • Live with wisdom and integrity,
  • Serve faithfully in church and society,
  • And glorify God in every sphere of life.

We believe the gospel of Jesus Christ is true, that God's Word is trustworthy, and that Christian education matters because Christ is Lord over all.

Our Pursuit

"Pursuing the Knowledge of God."

This is not merely our motto — it is our mission. To know God truly, to love Him deeply, and to teach the next generation that all wisdom begins and ends with Him.

Rooted in These Beliefs

Every program, policy, and practice at Colorado Heritage flows from this foundation. We invite families who share these convictions to join us.
