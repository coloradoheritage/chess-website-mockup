How It Works

Your Private School of Record

Under Colorado Revised Statute § 22-33-104.5, homeschooling families have a specific legal pathway to designate a private or parochial school — like Colorado Heritage — as their official school of record. When you enroll, CHESS assumes formal legal custody of your student's cumulative educational records: attendance logs, academic progress reports, standardized test results, and transcripts.

This means your family reports to us, not to the public school district. Colorado requires students to undergo standardized testing at grades 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11 — enrolled families submit those results directly to CHESS rather than to their local district. We serve as the official permanent repository for all educational progress records.

Enrollment in the CHESS Independent School also legally reclassifies your children. Instead of being registered as "homeschoolers" subject to district oversight, your students become private school students — a status shift that transfers educational accountability from public school officials to CHESS.

Colorado Legal Requirements

Compulsory attendance: age 6 by August 1st

Minimum: 172 days / 688 hours per school year

Testing required at grades: 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11 — submitted to CHESS, not the district

Authority: Colo. Rev. Stat. § 22-33-104.5(3)(f)–(g)

What CHESS Does For Your Family

  • Legal compliance — Fulfills Colorado Compulsory School Attendance Law on your behalf
  • Record custody — We are the official permanent repository for your student's educational records
  • Privacy protection — Your student's records are held by CHESS, not in public school district databases
  • Institutional credentialing — Official private school transcripts and diplomas that colleges and universities recognize
  • Test score reporting — Standardized test results go to CHESS, not your local district
  • HSLDA discount — 15% off HSLDA membership for all enrolled families

Important: Annual Re-Enrollment

Enrollment must be renewed every year. Families must submit required paperwork and updated records by the fall deadline. Failure to re-enroll may create truancy issues with your local district.

The Most Important Benefit

From "Homeschooler" to "Private School Student"

This is not just a label change. It is a fundamental shift in your legal relationship with the public school system.

Independent Homeschooler

  • Must report directly to the local public school district
  • Student's name and records may appear in district databases
  • Academic progress reviewed by district officials or district-approved evaluators
  • Subject to shifting district policies and priorities
  • Test scores submitted to and held by the local district

CHESS Private School Student

  • Reports to CHESS — a private Christian school — not the district
  • Records held privately by CHESS, not in public databases
  • Academic oversight stays within the private school relationship
  • Governed by CHESS policies, rooted in biblical conviction
  • Test scores submitted to and held by CHESS

Under Colorado law, this legal distinction means public school officials no longer have direct jurisdiction over your homeschool. You have chosen your accountability partner — and you have chosen one that shares your values, respects parental authority, and operates from a Christian worldview.
