Our Offerings

How We Support Home Educators

Independent School

Our Private Christian "umbrella school" services take the stress out of record-keeping, transcript formulation, and state reporting. We provide scheduling advice and encouragement to families pursuing the noble work of teaching at home.

How to Enroll

K-12 Academy

Over 30 years of providing quality Christ-centered classes. Elementary and junior high courses are varied, stimulating, and fulfilling. High schoolers can tackle rigorous academic courses alongside like-minded homeschoolers.

View Academy Classes

Diploma Program

A structured high school system offering four customized diploma levels (Diploma, Honors, High Honors, Highest Honors). Upon completion, students graduate with an esteemed transcript and walk in our beautiful ceremony.

Explore High School Diplomas
