Our Philosophy

God Has Given Parents the Responsibility to Lead

At Colorado Heritage, we believe that God has given parents the responsibility to lead the academic and spiritual education of their children. With this in mind, we have designed our Independent School, Academy, and other services with maximum academic flexibility while giving you the support and accountability you choose.

We are not a school that replaces the parent — we are a partner that strengthens and supports the family's mission.
