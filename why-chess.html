Why Choose CHESS?

Home Resources Why CHESS?

The Bottom Line

Exceptional Value. Uncompromised Values.

Public schools in Colorado spend an average of $13,000–$16,000 per student per year in taxpayer money — and yet many families feel they lack control over what their children are taught, how they are taught, and the values being instilled along the way.

Colorado Heritage exists to give homeschooling families the institutional structure, academic credibility, and Christ-centered community they need — at a fraction of the cost of private school, and with far more flexibility than either public or private alternatives.

Serving Families Everywhere

We Enroll Families in All 50 States

Colorado Heritage is a Colorado-based private school, but our Independent School and Diploma Program services are available to homeschooling families anywhere in the United States.

Families outside Colorado enroll under our private school umbrella for record-keeping, transcript services, and diploma program participation. You continue homeschooling in compliance with your own state's laws, while CHESS handles the institutional record-keeping.

Beyond Just Homeschooling

What CHESS Adds to Your Homeschool

Legal Compliance

Enrolling in CHESS fulfills Colorado's compulsory school attendance law. We handle attendance reporting so you don't have to interface with the public school district.

College-Ready Transcripts

Self-prepared homeschool transcripts are often viewed skeptically by college admissions offices. CHESS issues official private school transcripts that colleges recognize and respect.

Christian Community

Through Academy classes, choir, drama, and graduation, your student connects with a community of like-minded Christian homeschoolers — something hard to replicate on your own.

Expert Instruction

Our K–12 Academy offers classes taught by qualified instructors in subjects where a professional teacher adds real value — science, foreign language, writing, and more.

Accountability

A structured diploma plan with semester deadlines and required reporting keeps students on track through high school — providing the accountability that helps students succeed.

35 Years of Expertise

Since 1991, Colorado Heritage has guided thousands of homeschool families through every stage — from kindergarten enrollment to college-ready diplomas. We know this journey.
