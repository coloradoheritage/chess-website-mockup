The Bottom Line

Exceptional Value. Uncompromised Values.

Public schools in Colorado spend an average of $13,000–$16,000 per student per year in taxpayer money — and yet many families feel they lack control over what their children are taught, how they are taught, and the values being instilled along the way.

Colorado Heritage exists to give homeschooling families the institutional structure, academic credibility, and Christ-centered community they need — at a fraction of the cost of private school, and with far more flexibility than either public or private alternatives.

Serving Families Everywhere

Serving Homeschool Families Nationwide

Colorado Heritage is a Colorado-based private school. Families anywhere in the country can enroll for record-keeping support, official transcripts, and Diploma Program participation — services we provide regardless of where you live.

For families in Colorado, CHESS serves as your full Independent School of record and satisfies the state's compulsory-attendance requirements. Outside Colorado, how CHESS fits depends on your state's homeschool laws: some states allow an out-of-state private school like CHESS to serve as your school of record, while others ask families to register locally as homeschoolers and use CHESS as their record-keeping, transcript, and diploma partner. The rules — and the role accreditation plays — differ from state to state.

Important Note for Out-of-State Families

We strongly encourage families outside Colorado to contact our office before enrolling so we can help you understand how CHESS fits within your state's specific homeschool laws. We'll give you an honest answer.
