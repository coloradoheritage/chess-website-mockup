Home Programs Diploma Program

What It Is

An Official Private School Diploma Since 1991

The Colorado Heritage Diploma Program provides home-educated students with a structured four-year high school plan, official private school transcripts, and a homeschool diploma recognized by colleges and universities nationwide. We have been offering this program since 1991 — making us one of the longest-running homeschool diploma programs in Colorado.

With over 500 graduates and a 75% college or university attendance rate, our Diploma Program has a proven track record of preparing students for life after high school. Students combine Academy classes with home study to meet diploma requirements under the supervision of CHESS faculty.

When your student graduates, they receive an official transcript from Colorado Heritage Education School System — a private Christian school — that colleges, employers, and military branches recognize and respect.

Diploma Levels

Four Paths to Graduation

Students earn one of four diploma levels based on their academic achievement and coursework. All four levels result in an official CHESS private school transcript.

D

Diploma

Standard Level

Awarded to students who complete the CHESS standard course of study, fulfilling all required credits in core subjects over four years. Recognized as an official private school diploma for college admissions, employment, and military service.

Requirements: Complete standard CHESS credit requirements in English, math, science, history, Bible, and electives.
H

Honors Diploma

Honors Level

Awarded to students who meet the standard diploma requirements and additionally complete a specified number of Honors-designated courses with qualifying grades, demonstrating a higher level of academic achievement.

Requirements: Standard diploma credits plus completion of Honors courses at required grade thresholds.
HH

High Honors Diploma

High Honors Level

Awarded to students who exceed the Honors requirements by completing a greater number of advanced courses and maintaining high academic standing throughout their four-year program. Recognized on the official CHESS transcript.

Requirements: Honors diploma requirements plus additional advanced coursework and sustained academic excellence.
Highest Achievement
★

Highest Honors Diploma

Summa Level

The pinnacle of academic achievement in the CHESS Diploma Program. Awarded to students who complete the most rigorous course of study with distinction, demonstrating exceptional commitment to academic and spiritual formation throughout their high school years.

Requirements: High Honors requirements plus the highest credit totals, grade thresholds, and demonstrated excellence across all subject areas.

How the Diploma Program Works

1

Enroll

Apply to CHESS and enroll in both the Independent School and Diploma Program.

2
