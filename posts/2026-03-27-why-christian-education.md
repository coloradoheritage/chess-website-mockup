This year marks 35 years of Colorado Heritage Education School System — and we are deeply grateful to God for His faithfulness over every one of them.

## Why Christian Education?

The question is worth asking plainly. Why should a family choose Christian education? Why build an entire school system around biblical conviction?

The answer begins with a simple premise: **all truth is God's truth.** Education is never neutral. Every curriculum, every worldview, every approach to learning carries assumptions about the nature of reality, the purpose of human beings, and the source of moral authority. Christian education simply makes those assumptions explicit — and anchors them in Scripture.

We believe that God has entrusted the education of children to their parents. Not to the state, not to institutions, and not to culture. To parents. CHESS exists to come alongside families who take that responsibility seriously.

## 35 Years of Faithfulness

When Colorado Heritage was founded in 1991, homeschooling was a fringe movement. Families who chose to educate at home did so with limited support, uncertain legal standing, and very few resources. CHESS was built to change that — to give homeschooling families a private school home, accountability, and community.

Thirty-five years later, we have served hundreds of families across Colorado, the United States, and internationally. We have watched children grow into graduates, graduates grow into parents, and parents return to enroll their own children.

That is God's provision. We do not take it for granted.

## Looking Forward

As we enter our 36th year, our commitment remains unchanged: to partner with families in the noble pursuit of a Christ-centered education. To provide structure without rigidity, accountability without control, and community without compromise.

Thank you for being part of this story.

*"Pursuing the Knowledge of God."*
