When parents begin looking at educational options, they often ask, "What is the best curriculum?" or "What is the best school?" While those questions matter, research consistently shows that one factor has a greater impact on a child's success than almost anything else: parental involvement.

Children thrive when parents take an active role in their education. They tend to perform better academically, develop stronger character, and become more confident and responsible adults. For Christian families, this principle goes even deeper.

Scripture places the responsibility for teaching and discipling children primarily on parents. Deuteronomy 6:7 commands parents to teach God's truth diligently to their children throughout daily life. Education is not simply about academics; it is about forming young men and women who know, love, and serve Christ.

This is one reason many families choose homeschooling. Homeschooling allows parents to remain closely involved in their children's learning while tailoring education to their unique needs. However, many parents also desire academic support, classroom experiences, and Christian community.

![A child writing in a notebook with an open mathbook](https://coloradoheritage.github.io/chess-website-mockup/images/uploads/gemini_generated_image_8kr1lt8kr1lt8kr1.png "Learning at home")

That's where CHESS comes in.

CHESS offers a two-day-per-week hybrid homeschool program designed to support parents, not replace them. Students receive quality instruction and meaningful peer interaction while parents remain the primary educators and influence in their children's lives.

By combining the strengths of homeschooling with the benefits of a Christian learning community, CHESS helps families maintain what matters most: active parental involvement.

At the end of the day, successful education isn't just about what children learn. It's about who they become—and parents remain the most important influence in that process.
