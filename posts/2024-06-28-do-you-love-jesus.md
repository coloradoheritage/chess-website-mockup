It is a simple question. But it is the question that everything else depends on.

## The First Thing

In our zeal to provide excellent education — rigorous academics, strong transcripts, well-prepared graduates — it is possible to lose sight of the first thing. Jesus asked Peter three times: *"Do you love me?"* He did not ask: *"Are your students prepared for college?"* He did not ask: *"Are your records in order?"*

He asked: *"Do you love me?"*

We believe that Christian education begins and ends with this question. Not because academics don't matter — they do. Not because structure and accountability are unimportant — they are essential. But because all of it flows from this: a genuine love for Jesus Christ that shapes how we see the world, how we teach our children, and why we do what we do.

## For the Parents

You are not simply a curriculum manager. You are a shepherd. The children in your home are watching you — not just what you teach, but how you live. They are learning whether faith is a compartment or a foundation.

This is not a burden. It is the most important work in the world.

## For Our Community

At Colorado Heritage, we want to be more than a record-keeping service or an academic program. We want to be a community of families who are genuinely pursuing the knowledge of God together — who encourage one another, pray for one another, and hold one another accountable to the first thing.

Do you love Jesus? Let everything else flow from there.
