A Milestone Worth Celebrating

The CHESS Graduation Ceremony

Colorado Heritage hosts an annual graduation ceremony that is one of the most meaningful milestones in a homeschool student's life. It is a formal, Christ-centered celebration — complete with processional, Scripture readings, a commencement address, and diploma presentation.

Families and friends gather to witness the culmination of years of dedicated work. For many students, walking across the stage at CHESS graduation is a memory they will carry for a lifetime.

Who Can Participate?

Diploma Program Members

Students who have completed all requirements for a CHESS Diploma (any level) are eligible to participate in the graduation ceremony.

Fee: $150.00

Independent School Members

Families enrolled in the CHESS Independent School (but not the Diploma Program) may participate in the graduation ceremony as non-diploma walkers.

Fee: $175.00

Non-Members

Homeschool students not currently enrolled at Colorado Heritage may participate in the graduation ceremony on a case-by-case basis.

Fee: $200.00

* Fees are subject to change. Please view the full fee schedule or contact our office to confirm current rates.

Ceremony Details

Timing

The graduation ceremony is held annually, typically in late spring. Contact our office for this year's specific date and time.

Location

The ceremony is held at a local church venue in the Greeley, Colorado area. Remote and out-of-state families are warmly welcomed — many travel specifically for this occasion.

Cap & Gown

Graduates wear cap and gown for the processional and ceremony. Specific details on ordering cap and gown will be provided upon graduation registration.

Program

The ceremony includes processional, opening prayer, Scripture reading, worship, a commencement address, individual diploma presentations, and a recessional.

Diploma Presentation

Each graduate's name is called individually. Diplomas are presented on stage. Official transcripts are available separately — see our fee schedule.

Photography

Professional photography is available. Families are also welcome to take photos during the ceremony. View past ceremony photos in our Photo Gallery.
