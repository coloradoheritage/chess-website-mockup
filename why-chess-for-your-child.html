The Case for CHESS

Why Choose CHESS for Your Child

A Christ-centered enrichment Academy that strengthens homeschooling families — giving your child devoted teachers, godly community, and a biblical foundation, without ever taking your place as the parent.

The Question Every Parent Asks

You Are the Most Important Influence in Your Child's Life

When families weigh their options, they usually ask, "What is the best curriculum?" or "What is the best school?" Those questions matter — but research, and Scripture, point to something deeper. The single greatest factor in a child's growth is not a program or a building. It is an engaged, faithful parent.

Children flourish when their parents stay close to their learning, their character, and their walk with God. For a Christian family, this is not merely good practice — it is a calling. God placed the education and discipleship of children first in the hands of mothers and fathers (Deuteronomy 6:6–7), not the state.

So the real question is not whether you should stay involved. It is how you get the support you need without surrendering your role. That is exactly what Colorado Heritage was built to do.

Help Without the Handover

The Enrichment Academy

Every family we serve is supported first by our Independent School umbrella — the record-keeping and accountability that covers all homeschoolers. The Enrichment Academy is an optional next step on top of that foundation.

It's a way for CHESS to come alongside you with classes in the subjects that are hardest to teach at home — taught by people who care deeply about Christian education — one or two days a week, without ever taking over the teaching. You remain the primary teacher; we simply lighten the load and enrich what you're already doing.

Teachers Who Care Deeply

Sciences with real labs, writing, literature, logic, languages, music, and art — taught by men and women who love Christian education and gladly give their time and talents to help children learn and prepare for college. Many are veteran homeschool parents who have walked this road themselves.

Every Subject, Christ-Centered

We do not bolt a Bible verse onto a secular lesson. Because all truth is God's truth, every subject — from history to chemistry — is taught in light of God's character and created order.

Godly Community & Friendships

Your child learns and grows alongside like-minded families. Lasting friendships, encouragement, and accountability form in a setting that shares your values — not one that works against them.

A Two-Day Model That Protects Your Home

Classes meet just one or two days a week. That deliberate rhythm keeps you at the center of your child's education and leaves room for family life, discipleship, travel, and real-world learning.

Something for Every Stage

From kindergarten through twelfth grade, classes are organized by grade band so each student is challenged and encouraged at the right level — across two 14-week semesters each year.

A Partner, Not a Replacement

We never aim to take your place. Our role is to strengthen what you are already doing — relieving the pressure of teaching every subject alone while keeping you firmly in charge.

More Than a Co-op

A Complete System Behind Your Family

The Academy is the heart of CHESS — but it sits inside a complete support system. Whether you need legal covering, a recognized diploma, or testing, the same Christ-centered team stands behind you at every stage.

Independent School

Enroll under CHESS as your private school of record. We handle record-keeping and accountability so your family meets Colorado's requirements with confidence — and enrolled families receive a 15% discount on HSLDA membership.

Learn more

Diploma Program

A customizable four-year high school plan with four diploma tracks, an official transcript, and a graduation ceremony. Since 1991 we have graduated over 500 students, with about 75% continuing on to college or university.

Learn more

Standardized Testing

Convenient achievement testing and assessment options for your students — helpful for tracking progress, meeting evaluation needs, and preparing for the years ahead.

The Difference That Matters Most

Education Is the Formation of a Whole Person

Every classroom teaches a worldview — the only question is which one. Public and secular settings are not neutral; they shape how a child sees God, truth, and themselves. At CHESS, we are honest about our foundation: Jesus Christ is Lord over all of life, and education exists to help students know, love, and serve Him.

The goal is not merely academic achievement or a strong transcript, though we pursue both with excellence. The goal is to raise young men and women of wisdom, character, and faith — prepared to serve Christ in every calling He places before them.

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it."

— Proverbs 22:6

Is CHESS Right for You?

CHESS Is a Good Fit If You Want…

To remain the primary teacher and shepherd of your children — with caring help in the subjects that are hardest to teach at home.

A genuinely Christ-centered education, where Scripture is the foundation rather than an add-on.

Christian community and friendships for your children — and fellowship with like-minded parents for you.

Accountability, record-keeping, a recognized diploma, and testing — all from one trusted partner who has served families since 1991.

Give Your Child the Best of Both Worlds

The freedom and closeness of homeschooling, strengthened by a Christ-centered Academy and a team that has walked alongside families for over 35 years. We would love to help you take the next step.
