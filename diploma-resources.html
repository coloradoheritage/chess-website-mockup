Getting Started

Planning Your High School Years

The CHESS Diploma Program provides a structured, four-year high school plan that combines Academy classes with home study. Students graduate with an official CHESS transcript recognized by colleges and universities.

Use the resources below to understand diploma requirements, plan your coursework, and prepare for graduation. Questions? Contact our office — we're here to help.

Diploma Levels & Requirements

D

Diploma

The standard CHESS diploma, earned by completing the required credits across core academic subjects over four years of high school.

  • Bible / Worldview: 4 credits
  • English / Language Arts: 4 credits
  • Mathematics: 3 credits
  • Science: 2 credits
  • History / Social Studies: 3 credits
  • Electives: varies
H

Honors Diploma

Builds on the standard diploma with additional academic rigor, including a foreign language requirement and higher math expectations.

  • All Diploma requirements, plus:
  • Foreign Language: 2 credits
  • Mathematics through Algebra II
  • Science: 3 credits (one lab science)
HH

High Honors Diploma

Designed for college-bound students seeking a highly rigorous academic track, including advanced coursework across all subjects.

  • All Honors requirements, plus:
  • Foreign Language: 3 credits
  • Mathematics through Pre-Calculus
  • Fine Arts: 1 credit
★

Highest Honors Diploma

The highest academic distinction awarded by CHESS. Students pursuing this diploma complete an exceptionally rigorous four-year academic plan.

  • All High Honors requirements, plus:
  • Mathematics through Calculus
  • Foreign Language: 4 credits
  • Senior thesis or capstone project

* Credit requirements are approximate. Contact our office to confirm exact requirements for your diploma level before planning your coursework.

Transcripts & College Preparation

Official Transcripts

CHESS issues official transcripts for all Diploma Program students. Transcripts are available electronically or by mail. Fees apply — see our fee schedule.

College Recognition

Our private school transcripts are recognized for college admissions. We recommend students also take the SAT, ACT, or CLT (offered through CHESS) to strengthen their applications.

Reporting Deadlines

Coursework must be reported on time each semester. Late reporting fees apply. Contact our office for current semester deadlines and how to submit home study records.
