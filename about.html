Section 01

Our Purpose

Colorado Heritage Education School System (CHESS) is a private Christian school designed to meet the needs of homeschooling families throughout Colorado, across the United States, and internationally.

Established in 1991, we have provided education support for over 35 years, positioning ourselves as a partner with families seeking God-centered academic instruction through three primary offerings: Independent School, Diploma Program, and K–12 Academy.

We serve families who believe that education is not simply the transmission of facts, but the formation of the whole person — mind, heart, and character — in the light of Scripture.

What We Believe

  • God has entrusted the education of children to their parents, not the state.

  • Academic rigor and biblical truth are not in tension — they belong together.

  • Homeschooling families deserve institutional support, accountability, and community.

  • Serving families is a ministry — and we approach it as such.

"Pursuing the Knowledge of God."

Section 02

Our Foundation

Every aspect of Colorado Heritage is built on a biblical understanding of education, family, and faith.

Scripture as Authority

The Bible is the foundation of all truth. Our programs and policies are informed by Holy Scripture and our organizational position statements.

Family as the Unit

We seek students from families who are conforming to the biblical pattern for family found in Holy Scripture. We serve families led by a husband and wife, a single parent, or legal guardians.

Integrity in All Things

From enrollment paperwork to transcripts and diplomas, we operate with the highest standards of accuracy, accountability, and trustworthiness.

Acceptance & Eligibility

Our Acceptance Policy

The Colorado Heritage Independent School, Academy, and all programs seek students from families conforming to the biblical pattern for family found in Holy Scripture and specified in the various position statements of this organization.

Applications are considered from families led by a husband and wife (man and woman), a single parent, or legal guardians.

Section 03

Our History

Colorado Heritage was established in 1991 — more than 35 years ago — to serve the growing community of homeschooling families in Colorado who needed private school accountability, academic support, and community.

What began as a local umbrella school has grown into a full-service private Christian school system serving families across Colorado, the United States, and internationally through our Independent School, K–12 Academy, and Diploma Program.

We are thankful for God's provision and faithfulness over these decades, and for every family who has trusted us to walk alongside them in the noble pursuit of educating their children.

91

1991 — Founded

Colorado Heritage established in Greeley, CO to serve homeschooling families as a private Christian independent school.

Academy Launched

K–12 Academy classes begin, expanding beyond umbrella school services to in-person instruction for homeschooling families.

Diploma Program Added

High school students gain access to a four-year plan culminating in a private Christian school diploma and graduation ceremony.

Now

2026 — 35+ Years Strong

Serving families in Colorado, across the US, and internationally with three programs and a growing community.

Section 04

Our Philosophy

At Colorado Heritage, we believe that God has given parents the responsibility to lead the academic and spiritual education of their children.

With this in mind, we have designed our Independent School, Academy, and other services with maximum academic flexibility while giving you the support and accountability you choose.

We partner with families to pursue excellence in education and faith — not to replace the parent, but to walk alongside them in one of the most important callings in a family's life.
