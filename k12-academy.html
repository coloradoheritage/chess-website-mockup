What It Is

Homeschool Enrichment Classes in Greeley, CO

The Colorado Heritage K–12 Academy is a homeschool co-op offering in-person, Christ-centered classes for students in grades K–12. Families meet one or two days per week at our facility in Greeley, Colorado, combining the best of home-directed education with the richness of structured classroom instruction.

With over 35 years of experience serving homeschool families on the Front Range, our Academy provides a broad range of homeschool enrichment classes — from core academic subjects to music, drama, and the arts — all rooted in a biblical worldview.

Whether you're new to homeschooling or a seasoned family, Christian K–12 classes at Colorado Heritage offer the community, accountability, and academic excellence your student deserves.

What We Offer

Something for Every Stage of Learning

Elementary & Junior High

Grades K–8

Our K–8 program offers a variety of stimulating courses designed to spark curiosity, build a solid academic foundation, and foster love of learning within a Christian community. Classes range from language arts and science to art, music, and hands-on history.

  • Stimulating, varied course offerings
  • Christ-centered instruction throughout
  • Peer community and social development
  • Complements your home curriculum

High School Courses

Grades 9–12

High school students benefit from rigorous academics taught by qualified instructors. Our college-prep courses challenge students to think critically, write clearly, and engage deeply with ideas — all within a biblical framework that prepares them for life beyond graduation.

  • Rigorous academic courses
  • College preparation emphasis
  • Qualified subject instructors
  • Pairs with our Diploma Program

Community & Fellowship

All Grades

One of the greatest gifts we offer homeschool families is community. Students build lasting friendships, participate in choir, drama productions, and other co-curricular activities, and celebrate milestones together — including a beautiful graduation ceremony honoring our seniors.

  • Choir & choral performances
  • Drama productions
  • Graduation ceremony
  • Lifelong friendships

Why Choose CHESS

More Than Just Classes

The CHESS Academy is not a school replacement — it's an enrichment partner for your homeschool. You remain your child's primary educator, setting the direction and scope of their education. We provide expert instruction, peer community, and activities that would be difficult to replicate at home alone.

Our instructors are Christians who integrate faith throughout their teaching, not as an add-on, but as the foundation. Students are treated as image-bearers of God and taught to see all learning as connected to their relationship with Him.
