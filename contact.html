New Families

Apply to CHESS

We welcome families who share our commitment to Christ-centered education and who are ready to partner with us in the pursuit of academic excellence and biblical truth.

Enrollment in our programs is done on a yearly basis. Returning families must re-apply each school year and submit required paperwork by the published deadline.

1

Review Our Programs

Explore the Independent School, K–12 Academy, and Diploma Program to determine which is right for your family.

2

Contact Our Office

Reach out with questions or to schedule an appointment. We're happy to walk you through the process.

3

Complete Your Application

Submit your application through our New Applicant Portal and provide required documentation.
