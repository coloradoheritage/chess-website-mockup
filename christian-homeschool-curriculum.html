Christian Homeschool Curriculum Guide

Subject-by-subject recommendations for K–12 Christian homeschool families, organized by approach and grade level. Compiled from 35 years of serving homeschool families.

Note: Colorado Heritage does not officially endorse any specific curriculum. The recommendations below are provided as a starting point based on common choices among our families and the broader Christian homeschool community. Every family's needs are different — pray, research, and choose what fits your children and your calling.

Choosing Your Educational Approach

Before choosing specific curriculum materials, it helps to understand the major educational philosophies. Most families end up with a blend, but knowing the main approaches helps you make informed choices.

Classical Education

Based on the Trivium: Grammar (knowledge), Logic (understanding), and Rhetoric (application/expression). Emphasizes great books, Latin, history as a narrative, and rigorous thinking. Aligns beautifully with Christian formation.

Best fit: Families who love great books, want strong reasoning skills, and value depth over breadth.

Traditional / Textbook

Uses textbooks, workbooks, and structured lesson plans similar to a conventional school. Many Christian publishers (Abeka, Bob Jones, Sonlight) produce full-year curricula for each grade.

Best fit: Families who want clear structure, measurable progress, and straightforward lesson planning.

Charlotte Mason

Emphasizes "living books" over dry textbooks, nature study, narration (oral and written), short focused lessons, and respect for the child as a person. Rich, gentle, and deeply formative.

Best fit: Families who love beauty, nature, and literature-rich learning. Especially good for younger children.

Eclectic / Unit Studies

Combines methods and organizes learning around topics or units. Very flexible and can integrate multiple subjects around a central theme (e.g., ancient history, space exploration).

Best fit: Multi-age families or parents who enjoy creative lesson planning and mixing and matching resources.

Bible & Worldview

Bible and Christian worldview should be the foundation of every subject, not just a separate class. But a dedicated Bible curriculum gives your student systematic grounding in Scripture, theology, and Christian thinking.

Apologia

K–12 · Apologetics

Excellent worldview and apologetics courses for all ages. Their "What We Believe" series (K–6) and "Who is God?" series are widely used in Christian homeschools.

Veritas Press Bible

K–8 · Classical

Scripture memory, Bible survey, and catechism integrated with a classical approach. Aligns well with classical history and literature programs.

Summit Ministries

High School · Worldview

"Understanding the Times" is a rigorous worldview curriculum comparing Christianity to secular worldviews. Essential preparation for college and culture.

Language Arts, Writing & Literature

The Well-Trained Mind

K–12 · Classical

The definitive guide to classical homeschooling. Recommends primary sources, grammar study, writing through the ages. Essential reading for any homeschool parent.

Institute for Excellence in Writing (IEW)

K–12 · Writing

The gold standard for structured writing instruction in the homeschool community. Andrew Pudewa's methods are clear, effective, and used by thousands of Christian homeschool families.

Sonlight

K–12 · Literature-Based

Literature-rich, Charlotte Mason-influenced curriculum packages. Uses real books instead of textbooks. Excellent for building a love of reading alongside academic rigor.

All About Reading / Spelling

K–3 · Phonics

Highly recommended Orton-Gillingham based phonics and spelling program. Excellent for early readers and students who struggle with traditional reading instruction.

Classical Conversations

K–12 · Classical Memory

Memory-based classical program with a strong community component. Children meet weekly in co-op groups, memorizing grammar, history timelines, math facts, science, and Scripture.

Abeka Language Arts

K–12 · Traditional

Traditional, structured, and explicitly Christian. Comprehensive phonics, grammar, and composition. One of the most widely used traditional homeschool programs.

Mathematics

Saxon Math

K–12 · Incremental

The most widely used homeschool math program. Incremental, mastery-based approach with continuous review. Trusted for building strong computational skills through high school calculus.

Math-U-See

K–12 · Mastery/Visual

Mastery-based with manipulatives and video instruction. Excellent for visual and kinesthetic learners. Each level focuses on one concept until it's mastered before moving on.

Art of Problem Solving

Middle / High School · Advanced

For mathematically gifted students. Deep problem-solving focus from pre-algebra through calculus. Used by competition math students and college-prep homeschoolers.

History & Social Studies

Christian homeschoolers have a unique opportunity to teach history as the story of God's providence — not a sequence of random events. The programs below approach history from a biblical framework.

Story of the World

K–8 · Narrative

Susan Wise Bauer's beloved 4-volume world history series for children. Narrative, engaging, and excellent for all ages. A staple of classical and Charlotte Mason homeschools.

Notgrass History

6–12 · Christian Narrative

Explicitly Christian narrative history for middle and high school. Integrates Bible, primary sources, and literature. Full-credit courses available in American and world history.

Veritas Press History

K–8 · Classical

Classical history cards and self-paced courses. Chronological, 4-year rotation through ancient, medieval, Renaissance/Reformation, and modern history. Excellent for memorization and sequencing.

Science

Apologia Science

K–12 · Creation-Based

The most popular Christian homeschool science curriculum. Young Earth creation-based, rigorous, and conversational in tone. Covers biology, chemistry, physics, and more through high school with lab components.

Elemental Science

K–8 · Classical

Aligns science instruction with classical history cycles. Well-organized, easy for parents to teach, and integrates well with other classical programs.

Bob Jones University Science

K–12 · Traditional

Traditional, textbook-based Christian science curriculum. Includes lab manuals for high school. Well-regarded for rigorous college preparation.

Supplement Your Curriculum

Where CHESS Academy Classes Fit In

No matter what curriculum approach you use at home, CHESS Academy classes are designed to complement your home instruction in subjects that benefit from a professional instructor, group interaction, or specialized materials.

Science Labs

Lab-based science classes provide the hands-on experiments that are difficult to replicate at home. Critical for college applications requiring documented lab credit.

Foreign Language

Spanish and other language classes taught by dedicated teachers. Live conversation practice and structured grammar are hard to replicate without a teacher.

Writing & Composition

Structured writing workshops with instructor feedback help students develop skills beyond what a workbook alone can provide.

Fine Arts & Drama

Choir, drama, and arts programs provide community, performance experience, and credit — plus memories that last a lifetime.
