Policies & Guidelines

K–12 Academy Policies

These policies apply to all Academy students and families. Please read each section carefully before the start of each semester.

The CHESS Academy operates on a semester schedule. The academic year is divided into two semesters: fall and spring.

Specific start and end dates, registration deadlines, and important calendar events are published each year prior to registration opening. Please refer to the current-year schedule for exact dates.

Students are expected to be enrolled for the full semester. Withdrawal mid-semester is subject to the Tuition and Withdrawal Policy below.

Full semester details, including specific dates, will be provided at registration each year. Contact our office for the current academic calendar.

Tuition for Academy classes is due at the time of registration unless a payment plan has been established with the office.

If a student withdraws from a class, tuition refunds are issued according to the following schedule:

  • Before the first day of class: Full refund less a registration fee
  • Within the first two weeks: Partial refund (varies by class)
  • After the second week: No refund issued

All withdrawal requests must be submitted in writing to the CHESS office. Please contact us for specific refund amounts.

Full tuition and fee details are available on the Tuition & Fees page. Contact our office for questions about your specific enrollment.

The Academy seeks students from families conforming to the biblical pattern for family found in Holy Scripture and specified in the various position statements of this organization.

Applications are considered from families led by a husband and wife (man and woman), a single parent, or legal guardians.

Students are expected to demonstrate respect for authority, fellow students, and the Christian values upheld by Colorado Heritage.

For our full acceptance policy, see the Acceptance Policy page.

Students attending Academy classes are expected to dress modestly and appropriately, reflecting the Christian values of Colorado Heritage and showing respect for their instructors and peers.

General guidelines include:

  • Clothing should be clean, neat, and in good repair
  • Modesty standards should be maintained — clothing should not be revealing or distracting
  • Graphic content on clothing should be appropriate for a Christian academic setting
  • Hats may be permitted at the instructor's discretion

Instructors reserve the right to ask students to change or cover clothing that does not meet these standards. Consistent violations may result in a conversation with parents.

Personal electronic devices (cell phones, tablets, smartwatches, etc.) must be kept silent and put away during class time unless explicitly permitted by the instructor for academic purposes.

Computers or tablets may be used for note-taking or class assignments when permitted by the instructor.

Colorado Heritage is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal electronics. Students bring devices at their own risk.

Repeated disruptions due to electronics may result in a student being asked to leave the device in the hallway or with parents for the class period.

If your student will be absent from a class, please notify the CHESS office and the instructor in advance whenever possible.

Absences should be reported by email to office@coloradoheritage.org or by calling (970) 346-0099.

It is the student's responsibility to obtain missed assignments and notes from classmates or the instructor. Make-up opportunities are at the instructor's discretion.

Excessive unexcused absences may affect a student's standing in the class and may require a meeting between parents and the instructor.

Colorado Heritage is a community, and we depend on the participation of our families to make our programs excellent. We are grateful for every family that helps make CHESS what it is.

Opportunities to serve include:

  • Volunteering during class days
  • Helping with Academy events, performances, and field trips
  • Providing snacks or refreshments on a rotating schedule
  • Serving in a leadership capacity with prior approval

We ask that all volunteers conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the values and standards of Colorado Heritage. Contact our office to learn about current volunteer needs.

Questions About Policies?

Our office is happy to clarify any of these policies or answer questions specific to your family's situation.
