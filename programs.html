Program 01

Independent School

"Umbrella school" services for homeschooling families

Colorado Heritage Education School System is a private Christian school within the State of Colorado. The Colorado Heritage Independent School serves homeschooling families who wish to educate at home under the auspices of a private school.

Attendance records, immunization records, and test scores are submitted by our Independent School families to Colorado Heritage instead of to a public school district. Enrollment in our Independent School fulfills Colorado Compulsory School Attendance Law.

The State of Colorado homeschool school year attendance requirements of 172 days / 688 hours must be fulfilled by our Independent School students.

Apply to Independent School

Why Homeschool Under an Independent School?

Every homeschooler must have accountability either to the state (via a public school district) or to a private independent school.

Homeschooling families who are members of a private independent school report attendance and other required records to their independent school instead of to the state — providing privacy and protection for your family.

Important: Annual Re-Enrollment

Enrollment and re-enrollment with the Colorado Heritage Independent School is done on a yearly basis. Families must submit required paperwork by the deadline each year. Failure to do so means we cannot consider you as enrolled for that school year — which may create truancy issues.

Program 03

Diploma Program

A four-year plan for home-educated high school students

The Colorado Heritage Diploma Program provides home-educated high school students with a four-year plan that challenges them academically and spiritually. Students combine coursework through the Academy with home study.

Upon completion, students receive private Christian school transcripts and may participate in a graduation ceremony with their peers.

Four Diploma Levels

What's Included

  • Both academic and spiritual formation throughout high school

  • Access to co-curricular activities and fellowship opportunities

Resource

Colorado Homeschool Law

Understanding your legal rights and responsibilities as a homeschooling family in Colorado is essential. Enrolling in the Colorado Heritage Independent School is one of the primary ways families fulfill their legal obligations.

Key Colorado Homeschool Requirements

Compulsory Age

Age 6 by August 1st

Annual Requirement

172 days / 688 hours

Accountability Options

Report to a public school district or enroll in a private independent school like CHESS

Records Required

Attendance, immunization records, and test scores submitted annually

Additional Legal Resources

HSLDA (Homeschool Legal Defense Association) is a trusted resource for understanding and navigating homeschool law. As a CHESS Independent School family, you receive a 15% discount on HSLDA membership.
