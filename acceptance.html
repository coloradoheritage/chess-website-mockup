Acceptance & Eligibility Policy

Policy for acceptance in and eligibility for continued enrollment in our Independent School, Academy, High School Diploma Program, and other school-sponsored programs.

Home About Acceptance Policy

Policy Statement

Our Acceptance & Eligibility Policy

Colorado Heritage Education School System, a Private Christian School, seeks to honor and bring glory to God Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and to Whom all owe praise and worship for His Holiness, Justice, Goodness, Truth, and Mercy as is clearly communicated in His Word — the 66 books of the Holy Scriptures.

Colorado Heritage seeks students from families who are conforming to the biblical pattern for family as found in Holy Scripture and as specified in the various position statements of this organization.

Who May Apply

Applications Are Welcomed From Families Led By

Married Couple

A married husband and wife (one man and one woman)

Single Parent

A single parent raising their child in the home

Legal Guardian

A legal guardian or custodial family member

Eligibility for Continued Enrollment

Living as One's Biological Sex at Birth

To be eligible for acceptance and for continuing enrollment in Colorado Heritage, parents/guardians and students who apply for — and who are enrolled in — our Academy, Independent School, and/or Diploma Program must live their life consistent with their biological sex — the sex (male or female) determined by the chromosomes the Lord God sovereignly gave them before they were in their mother's womb.

The Scriptural Basis — Psalm 139:16

"Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them."

— Psalm 139:16 (KJV)

Our Christian Foundation

As a Christian educational institution, the school affirms historic Protestant Christian doctrine and seeks to maintain a learning environment consistent with biblical teaching, Christian character, moral integrity, personal responsibility, and mutual respect.

Every aspect of Colorado Heritage — from enrollment policies to curriculum philosophy — flows from a commitment to the authority and sufficiency of Scripture.

Scripture

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it."

— Proverbs 22:6

Admission Decisions

What We Consider

Admission decisions are made after considering the following factors:

Mission Alignment

The family's willingness to support the mission and policies of the school

Student Readiness

The student's academic and behavioral readiness

Program Fit

Available resources and program fit for the student's needs

Capacity to Serve

The school's ability to effectively serve the student and family

A Cooperative Partnership

Enrollment is considered a cooperative partnership between the school and the home. Families are expected to support the school's statement of faith, code of conduct, educational standards, and policies while their child is enrolled.

The school reserves the right to deny or discontinue enrollment when the partnership between the school and the family no longer supports the mission, policies, or biblical convictions of the institution.

Our Commitment to Every Family

The school is committed to treating every student and family with dignity, compassion, fairness, and genuine care as those made in the image of God. We desire to serve every enrolled family with excellence, integrity, and faithfulness in all matters relating to education and student life.

Non-Discrimination Statement

Colorado Heritage Education School System admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at Colorado Heritage. It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national and ethnic origin in administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and loan programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.
