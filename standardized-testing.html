Colorado Law

Testing Is Required for Colorado Homeschoolers

Colorado state law requires that homeschool students enrolled in an independent school (like CHESS) undergo standardized testing every other year, beginning in 3rd grade. Specifically, testing is required in grades 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11.

Colorado Heritage offers homeschool standardized testing services to our enrolled families, providing a convenient, low-stress way to fulfill this requirement while keeping your student's records with a trusted private school.

We offer multiple testing formats — private sessions, group sessions, and at-home testing — so you can choose what works best for your family. Contact our office for current testing dates and availability.

FAQ

Testing Questions Answered

Colorado law requires homeschool students enrolled in an independent school to undergo standardized testing every other year starting in 3rd grade. This applies to students in grades 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11. Families enrolled in CHESS are responsible for ensuring their students test in the required years.

Testing must be completed within the applicable school year. Colorado Heritage typically offers testing sessions throughout the spring semester. Contact our office early to secure your preferred session date, as spots are limited — especially for popular group sessions.

We offer the CORE battery (reading, language arts, math), the COMPLETE battery (CORE plus science, social studies, and reference skills), and the CLT (Classic Learning Test) for high school students. Tests can be taken privately, in a group session, or at home depending on your preference and the test type.

The Classic Learning Test (CLT) is designed for high school students as an alternative to the SAT or ACT for college admissions. It is accepted by hundreds of colleges and universities and is particularly popular in classical and Christian homeschool communities. The CLT tests verbal reasoning, grammar and writing, and quantitative reasoning from a classical academic perspective.

Yes. Colorado law allows testing through an independent school or through another qualified test administrator. However, CHESS families who use our testing service benefit from the convenience of having results kept directly in their student's CHESS records file.
